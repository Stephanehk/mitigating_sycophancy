Sycophancy user study — results

Values are scale positions, not raw choice ids — objectivity, quality and right-or-wrong skip ids in the source instrument, so raw codes would misstate the magnitudes. Each cell shows the mean with the half-width of its 95% confidence interval (1.96·SE), the interval Cheng et al. use in the source study. Bold marks the largest mean in a row, and largest is not best: on “right or wrong” a high score means “I was completely right”, and on chatbot-use frequency, 5 means “never”. The neutral model (n=1), ours — no editing of model input (n=3) are greyed as too small to read as estimates; an arm of one is also excluded from the bold comparison, and cells with one observation carry no interval, since a standard deviation is undefined there.

62 eligible participants of 145 submissions, collected 31 July 2026 – 2 August 2026. Generated from study_submissions_rows.csv by study/analysis/build_results_artifact.py, so every figure is reproducible and none was transcribed; question wording is read from study/questionnaires.py. Nothing is imputed, and nothing is filtered on the attention checks.

The run spans a design change: until 2026-08-01 assignment drew from all six arms, and from then on only from ours, the untrained model and the sycophantic model. So neutral model, ours — no editing of model input, untrained model — no editing of model input are no longer recruited, and the three live arms hold unequal numbers because part of their data was collected under the six-way draw.

95% confidence intervals follow Cheng et al., Sycophantic AI Decreases Prosocial Intentions and Promotes Dependence (arXiv:2510.01395), which reports “95% confidence intervals (1.96±SE)”: half-width = 1.96·sd/√n. At these cell sizes the normal approximation is slightly narrower than a t-based interval would be, so the intervals shown are, if anything, optimistic. They are also unadjusted for multiplicity — with roughly 600 available comparisons, individual intervals excluding zero should not be read as findings.

Built with --only tables: the participants’ own writing and the replies they saw are deliberately excluded, because the consent form promised anonymisation by removal of proper nouns before quotation. Every number here is an aggregate over participants; no free text appears on this page. The full version, including the response cards, stays with the research team.
